# ADR 0018 — Phase 5 purchasing, inventory and waste

- Date: 2026-10-01
- Status: accepted (built unattended — **§5, §6 and §9 are choices the founder should confirm**)

## Context
Phase 5 adds the minimum inputs that make food cost true: purchases, a weekly count, a waste log and transfers. METRICS.md "Inventory" defines the maths but leaves several practical rules open (what "usage" is measured in, when a count happens, where unit cost comes from once purchases exist, what a discipline point means on a day nothing was due). The seed needed a stock ledger so the Phase 5–6 scenarios (S6–S9) can be recovered exactly.

## Decision
1. **Usage is bought quantity.** `usage.theoretical` explodes each sold line through its recipe as qty ÷ yield — a 90 g line at 90% yield uses 100 g, the same rule `recipe_unit_cost` prices. Sub-recipes are walked down (five levels). A sold line uses the recipe its sale-time cost snapshot names, else the item's active recipe. Menu-item waste explodes the same way.
2. **A count on day D is D's closing stock.** `stock.theoretical` starts from each ingredient's latest submitted count on or before the date and adds everything after it. Never counted = unknown (shown as "not counted yet"), never zero. A later count on the same day supersedes an earlier one. Transfers leave on the day they are sent and arrive, at the received quantity, on the day they are confirmed; a sent-but-unconfirmed transfer is shown as "on the way" and is not in either branch's stock.
3. **Variance runs between two counts.** Opening = latest count on or before `from`, closing = latest count after it and on or before `to`, per ingredient; a missed weekly count simply widens the period. `variance.qty` is exactly METRICS.md's `usage.actual − usage.theoretical − waste.logged` (so it is *unexplained* usage; open question 1 is resolved in Phase 6's bridge by naming it so). Value = qty × the ingredient's unit cost on the closing day.
4. **Unit cost from what was paid.** A confirmed purchase writes the ingredient's price history for its purchase day: the 14-day weighted average paid across the organisation (paid ÷ quantity, stored exactly), or the last price paid (`org_cost_settings.unit_cost_method`). Voiding a purchase recomputes that day. Recipe costs from that day on use it; earlier sales keep their snapshot. The seed loads purchases in bulk without this refresh, so seeded recipe costs stay the market price (S4 unchanged) and S8's overpayments show only in purchases.
5. **Purchases are money.** Only the owner and the branch's purchaser record, confirm or see purchases (ADR 0007); the owner alone voids, with a reason. Stock quantities derived from purchases are not money and reach every stock role. *Confirm: in many Bangladeshi restaurants the manager does the bazar — give that person the purchaser role as well (one person may hold several roles), rather than letting managers see prices.*
6. **AI purchase drafts transcribe, never calculate.** A photo of the chit or receipt, typed text, or the purchaser's dictated words go to the gateway, which returns each line as written with a 0–1 confidence per field. Code then converts units (হালি = 4), matches ingredients (exact name in either language, then bazar words like মুরগি/murgi → chicken, then a unique partial name — never a guess between two), and flags anything unsure. The draft sits in `purchase_drafts` and moves nothing until a person confirms it through `confirm_purchase_draft`. The photo itself is not stored. Voice uses the browser's speech recognition (Chrome sends that audio to Google's speech service); it is about groceries, not customers, and typing always works. *Confirm that browser dictation is acceptable, or that voice should wait for a server-side speech service.*
7. **The guided count is blind and short.** It suggests the org's N costliest ingredients by the last 28 days' usage value (N = `guided_count_size`, default 12) and never shows the expected quantity. Due on `org_settings.count_weekday` (default Sunday).
8. **Money columns are withheld, not refused.** The variance and waste metrics return quantities to every stock role and null ৳ columns unless `can_see_money` — decided in SQL, so a manager still sees "2.4 kg missing" without its price.
9. **Discipline score** (METRICS.md, per branch per business day): bazar 25 if any confirmed purchase is dated that day; waste 15 if anything was logged; cash close 20 if the day had shifts and all are closed; count 25 if the count was due and submitted, or full marks on a day with no count due; recipe coverage 15 if costed lines are ≥ 90% of net sales (or there were none). A day with no orders and no shifts is closed and not scored. *Confirm: a day with genuinely no bazar loses 25 — acceptable as a nudge, or should "no purchase needed" be recordable?*
10. **Fresh goods have their own cover bands.** Days of cover uses the per-ingredient overrides already in the schema; fresh goods bought every morning are healthy at one day (the demo sets green ≥ 1, red < 0.5).
11. **Every stock-moving table is written only by audited RPCs** (purchases, lines, counts, count lines, waste, transfers), like the POS tables (ADR 0014). `purchase_drafts` stays directly writable: a draft moves nothing.
12. **The seed's stock ledger** simulates each branch's physical stock for twelve weeks and writes only what people would record; the gap is exactly the plants (S6 unrecorded chicken transfers, S7 12% mutton over-portioning, S8 beef overpayment, S9 unlogged rice waste) plus realistic noise (1% normal loss, cooked-then-voided dishes, Mirpur not logging weekend waste, a two-week onion market rise, one missed count, one unconfirmed transfer). It uses its own random streams, so order data is byte-identical.

## Alternatives considered
- **Usage in recipe (net) quantity** — then a yield loss would show up as unexplained variance every day.
- **Per-branch unit cost** — more exact for chains buying separately, but S8-style overpayment would then hide inside that branch's own "normal" cost; org-wide keeps one cost per ingredient and lets Phase 6 compare branches.
- **Showing expected quantities on the count sheet** — faster counts, but people confirm the number they see.
- **Storing chit photos in Supabase Storage** — useful evidence; deferred until there is a retention rule for it.

## Consequences
- Food-cost truth now depends on four habits; the dashboard and the morning brief nudge whoever skipped one.
- Phase 6 builds the variance bridge, price watch and the leak detectors on these functions; the manifest already carries their ground truth.
- **Revisit** the weighted-average window and the discipline weights with pilot data.
